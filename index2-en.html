Israel-Georgia Real Estate and Business Tourism Forum

Forum and Organizers

On December 21, 2026, the Pullman Tbilisi Axis Towers hotel will host the annual awards ceremony for successful government, business, and media projects, organized by Business Government Contact (BGC International) and the Israel-Georgia Business Forum. The event is organized annually by Business Media Contact (BMC), an international business media holding company dedicated to promoting the growth of Georgian business, nation branding, and raising awareness of Georgia’s business climate in the international market. The organization assists Georgian companies in establishing connections with international partners, investors, and target audiences.

This year’s forum and awards ceremony are also organized by the Israel-Georgia Business House (IGBH), which promotes Georgian real estate and business tourism in the Israeli market and fosters trade and economic relations between the two countries. Additionally, IGBH provides marketing, sales management, comprehensive legal support, and PR services to Georgian and international companies to deepen and expand investment, brokerage, and business tourism ties.

Each year, IGBH and BMC promote Georgian business through international media channels, including those partnered with the Georgian National Tourism Administration.

Furthermore, this year, BMC and IGBH will showcase the Georgian market on the international stage across Europe, the Americas, the CIS, and the UAE.

04

Israel-Georgia Real Estate and Business Tourism Forum

Forum Objectives

The “Israel Georgia Business Forum” and “BGC International” awards ceremony aims to:

1. Promote Georgian business, business tourism, investment, trade and economic potential, and Georgia’s successful business climate, as well as government projects that have created an attractive environment for investors and helped position Georgia in international markets.

The forum also aims to support the integration of Georgian companies into the Israeli market, attract international business partners and investors, provide Georgian and international companies with a complete marketing and sales-management chain for investment portfolio management, and conduct comprehensive research and analysis of the country’s investment potential.

05

Israel-Georgia Real Estate and Business Tourism Forum

Forum Participants

2. The first part of the forum will bring together leading high-level professionals and companies from the real estate and business tourism sectors operating in both Israeli and international markets—including brokers, realtors, investors, business travel organizers, and import-export firms. Participants from Georgia will include leading construction and development firms, architectural and engineering companies, building material manufacturers, real estate companies, brokers and realtors, analysts and experts on the Georgian economy, banks, investment funds, and insurance companies.

Also invited are representatives from business tourism sectors of interest to the Israeli market—such as medical, eco, wellness, and balneological tourism, as well as wine and agrotourism—alongside representatives from embassies and international chambers of commerce operating in Georgia, relevant government agencies, and international business associations.

06

Israel-Georgia Real Estate and Business Tourism Forum

Forum Programme

The business forum will include Business Expo, Welcome Party, Workshop, Global Networking Relationship, B2B and B2C meetings with the target audience and brokerage tours. Advertising and demonstration areas of participating companies with banners, flyers and accessories will be presented. Companies will be able to conduct business presentations with the screening of advertising videos and press releases, as well as present themselves in interviews with leading Georgian and Israeli media

The forum will present areas of business tourism that are of interest to the Israeli market: medical tourism, Wellness, Eco- and balneological tourism, wine and agritourism.

07

Israel-Georgia Real Estate and Business Tourism Forum

Awards Ceremony

The second part of the event will feature the Business Government Contact BGC International awards ceremony, honoring successful government and business projects, leading companies, investment projects, international chambers of commerce, international importers and exporters, representatives of all areas of business tourism, and prominent media nominees. Company promotional videos and image projects will be presented. Reports and programs about forum participants produced in Georgian media will be translated into English, Russian, and Hebrew, shared with international media partners, and published on international tourism and real estate agency channels. The awards ceremony and forum recording will also be broadcast through Georgian and Israeli international media.

Project Selection Criteria

Within the forum, awards will be presented by nomination category, and no single winning company will be selected. Every nominee is chosen and presented for an award in recognition of their contribution to the development of their segment, implementation of innovation and technology, and their role as a sector participant demonstrating high standards and professionalism.

Selection criteria for submitted projects:

  1. 1

  8. 8

03

Israel-Georgia Real Estate and Business Tourism Forum

Forum and Organizers

On December 21, 2026, at the “Pullman Tbilisi Axis Towers” hotel, the annual awards ceremony for successful government, business, and media projects, Business Government Contact “BGC International” and the “Israel Georgia Business Forum”, will be held. The event is organized annually by Business Media Contact BMC, an international business media holding company that promotes the development of Georgian business, strengthens the country’s brand, and raises awareness of Georgia’s business climate in international markets. It supports Georgian companies in building connections with international partners, investors, and target audiences.

This year’s forum and awards ceremony are also organized by “Israel Georgia Business House” IGBH, which promotes Georgian real estate and all areas of business tourism in Israel and supports the development of trade and economic relations between the two countries. IGBH also provides Georgian and international companies with marketing, sales management, full legal support, and PR services aimed at strengthening and developing investment, brokerage, and business tourism relations.

IGBH and BMC promote Georgian business annually through international media formats, using international partner media channels of the Georgian National Tourism Administration.

Starting next year, IGBH will present successful companies from Georgia at Israel’s international real estate and business tourism exhibition.

04

Israel-Georgia Real Estate and Business Tourism Forum

Forum Objectives

The aims of the “Israel Georgia Business Forum” and “BGC International” awards ceremony are:

1. Promote Georgian business, business tourism, investment, trade and economic potential, and Georgia’s successful business climate, as well as government projects that have created an attractive environment for investors and helped position Georgia in international markets.

The forum also aims to support the integration of Georgian companies into the Israeli market, attract international business partners and investors, provide Georgian and international companies with a comprehensive marketing and sales management support for investment portfolio management, and conduct comprehensive research and analysis of the country’s investment potential.

05

Israel-Georgia Real Estate and Business Tourism Forum

Forum Participants

2. The first part of the forum will bring together leading real estate and business tourism professionals and companies active in the Israeli and international markets, including brokers, realtors, investors, tourism companies organizing business travel, and import-export companies.

From Georgia, participants will include leading construction and development companies, architectural and engineering firms, building-material manufacturers, representatives of real estate companies, brokers and realtors, Georgian economic analysts and experts, banks, investment funds, insurance companies, representatives of embassies and international chambers of commerce operating in Georgia, supporting government agencies, and international business associations.

06

Israel-Georgia Real Estate and Business Tourism Forum

Forum Programme

The business forum format will include a business expo, a welcome party, workshops, global networking, B2B and B2C meetings with target audiences, as well as brokerage tours. Participating companies will have promotional display areas featuring banners, flyers, and accessories. Companies will be able to present their businesses through promotional videos and press releases, as well as interview-format presentations with leading Georgian and Israeli media.

The forum will also present business tourism sectors of interest to the Israeli market, including medical, wellness, eco and balneological tourism, wine tourism, and agritourism.

07

Israel-Georgia Real Estate and Business Tourism Forum

Awards Ceremony

The second part of the event will feature the Business Government Contact BGC International awards ceremony, honoring successful government and business projects, leading companies, investment projects, international chambers of commerce, international importers and exporters, representatives of all areas of business tourism, and prominent media nominees. Company promotional videos and brand-building projects will be presented. Reports and programs about forum participants produced in Georgian media will be translated into English, Russian, and Hebrew, shared with international media partners, and published on international tourism and real estate agency channels. The awards ceremony and forum recording will also be broadcast through Georgian and Israeli international media.

08

Israel-Georgia Real Estate and Business Tourism Forum

Project Selection Criteria

Within the forum, awards will be presented by nomination category, and no single winning company will be selected. Every nominee is chosen and presented for an award in recognition of their contribution to the development of their segment, adoption of innovation and technology, and their role as a sector participant demonstrating high standards and professionalism.

Selection criteria for submitted projects:

  1. 1

    Contribution to national development and progress

  2. 2

    Implementation of new technologies and innovations

  3. 3

    Contribution to the development of the business sector

  4. 4

    Major investment project

  5. 5

    A project with investment potential that is attractive to investors and offers a product in demand on the international market

  6. 6

    Large-scale multifunctional and infrastructure project

  7. 7

    Contribution to sustainable, energy-efficient, and “green” economy projects and their development

  8. 8

    Public trust, reputation, and high professionalism
